## Measured result

The headline is a rotating interleaved A/B run with a warm model and warm
pipelines but a reset KV cache and a distinct uncached prompt in each of six
rounds. The machine remained under an unrelated background workload:

- K=1 median: 148.355 tokens/s.
- K=4 median: **250.655 tokens/s**.
- Paired K=4/K=1 throughput ratio: **1.692x**.
- Ratio IQR 1.635–1.711, MAD 0.027, range 1.489–1.726, n=6.

The earlier quiet published-subgroup measurement was 5.97 ms/token. Combining
that absolute with the independently measured argmax/GEMV and fresh-prompt
burst ratios gives about **3.4 ms/token / 295 tokens/s**. That number is a
projection, not a measurement; `measurements.json` labels it accordingly.

## Exactness boundary

K=1 and K=4 generated byte-identical text for six fresh prompts and four
quality prompts. `verification/corpus.json` records every message, expected
text, and SHA-256. This establishes deterministic greedy agreement for that
corpus. It does not claim full-logit identity or stochastic-sampling parity.

K=4 is enabled only when temperature is zero and there are no logprobs,
grammar/structured-output matcher, logit bias, custom logit processor, or
frequency/presence/repetition penalty. Every other request falls back to the
normal one-token WebLLM sampler. K=4 can delay streaming and cancellation by up
to four tokens; unused steps are removed from the KV cache.

## Rejected paths and tuning record

- Chromium WebNN/Core ML Qwen layers were slower than WebGPU and lacked a
  viable dynamic-cache full-model route.
- Naive single-workgroup GPU argmax regressed decode; two-stage chunk-256 won.
- Chunk sizes 64/128/512 and GEMV `TR=64/128` lost to chunk-256 / `TR=32`.
- Deferred decode cleanup was neutral/noisy and is disabled.
- Fusing embed + decode + argmax in one compiler function hit TVM dlight
  `decompose_reduction` invalid-block failure. Runtime-level K-step decode is
  the accepted workaround.
- K=8 did not improve the convert phase over K=4 and worsened stream
  granularity and some repair measurements.
- q4f16_1 was the measured speed/memory winner over q4f32, q0f16 and larger
  Qwen variants for this target.

Raw aggregate and per-round JSON, GPU timestamp profiles, layer probes, and
the complete WGSL inventory are retained in the workbench commit above.
